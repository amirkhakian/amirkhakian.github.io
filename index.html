About

I’m Amir Khakian. For the past six years I’ve cut brand films, technical 3D animations and documentaries — mostly remotely, for clients in Dubai, Sweden, Denmark and Tehran. Alongside freelance work, I lead the editing, VFX and motion department at Dream Motion Pictures.

I work in Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop, with DaVinci Resolve for the finish — and AI creative tools where they serve the cut. Sound and music are part of the cut, not an afterthought.
